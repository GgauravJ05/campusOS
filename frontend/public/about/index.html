What CampusOS does

Three groups use it, and each gets what they need.

6 shown

  • Find and join events

    Browse by category, see the seats left, reserve a spot and join the waitlist when it is full.

  • Reminders that matter

    A notice two days before and again two hours before every event you registered for.

  • Conflict-free venues

    Request a hall or lab and see the calendar. Two clubs can never hold the same room at the same time.

  • Attendance and feedback

    Mark who turned up and collect feedback after the event.

  • Clear approvals

    One inbox for pending requests, ordered by the rule you choose, with every decision recorded.

  • Reports and audit

    Venue use, club activity and attendance, exportable as CSV or PDF, plus a tamper-proof audit trail.
